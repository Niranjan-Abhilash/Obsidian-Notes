## Upstream Downstream

- Downstream speed = speed of boat + speed of stream
- Upstream speed = speed of boat  - speed of stream
#### Example

Q. A boat travels 30 km downstream and 20 km upstream in 5 hours. The speed of the stream is 2 km/h. Form the quadratic equation.

Ans.
		let the speed of the boat be x km/h
		
		$=> upstream speed = x - 2$
		$=> downstream speed = x+2$

$Time = distance / speed$

$$
	\frac{30}{x+2} + \frac{20}{x-2} = 5
$$
$$
	\implies \frac{30 x-60+20x+40}{x^{2}-4} = 5
$$
$$
	\implies 50x-20 = 5x^{2}-20
$$
$$
	\implies 5x^{2}-50x=0
$$
$$
	\implies x=0 \space\space\text{or}\space\space x = 10 
$$

## Men and work

- Work = rate $*$ time 
- If one man takes x days to complete work, the one day's work is $\frac{1}{x}$
#### Example

Q. A man and a woman together can complete a work in 12 days. The woman alone takes 10 days more than the man. Form the quadratic equation.

Ans.
	let days man take to complete work be x
	$$
		\implies \text{time taken by women} = x + 10
	$$
	Their combined one day work:
	$$
		\frac{1}{x} + \frac{1}{x+10} = \frac{1}{12}
	$$
	$$
		\implies \frac{x+10+x}{x(x+10)} = \frac{1}{12}
	$$
	$$
		\implies x^{2}+10x = 24x+120
		\implies x^{2} - 14x -120 = 0
	$$
	$$
		\therefore\space x = 20 \space or \space x= -6
	$$

## Tap

- Think of taps as worker
- Filling tap = $+1/x$ 
- Emptying tap = $-1/x$
#### Example

Q. A tap fills a tank in x hours. Another tap empties it in x + 6 hours. Together they fill the tank in 8 hours. Form the quadratic equation.

Ans. 

one hour filling = $1/x$
one hour emptying = $1/(x+6)$

$$
	\implies \frac{1}{x} - \frac{1}{x+6} = \frac{1}{8}
$$
$$
	\implies \frac{x+6 - x}{x(x+6)} = \frac{1}{8}
$$
$$
	x^{2}+6x = 48 \implies\underline{\underline{x^{2} + 6x-48 = 0 }}
$$
$$
	\therefore x = -3 \pm \sqrt{ 57 }
$$

| Taps                | 1/x ± 1/(x+a) = tank filling rate      |
