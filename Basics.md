# Light and heavy syllables

_Generally_ **Heavy syllables** are:
- Syllables with long vowels.
- Syllables that are followed by multiple consonants.
- Syllables that are followed by the anusvāra or visarga.

all other syllables are light

# Sandhi

*सीता अश्वम्*  इच्छति → *सीताश्वम्*  इच्छति

Eg: गजो -> गजस् तरुं

> Generally, sandhi makes it easier to speak quickly and smoothly.
