##### Examples of tense changes


| Direct speech | Reported speech                        |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Will          | Would                                  |
| Can           | Could                                  |
| May           | Might                                  |
| Must          | Had to (sometimes remains must itself) |

### Pronoun changes

| Direct speech | Reported speech                  |
| ------------- | -------------------------------- |
| I             | Changes according to the subject |
| You           | Changes according to the object  |
| We            | Changes according to the speaker |

##### Example
- Riya told me, "You are late!"
- Riya told me that **I was late**
## Time and place changes

|Direct|Reported|
|---|---|
|now|then|
|today|that day|
|tomorrow|the next day / the following day|
|yesterday|the previous day|
|last night|the previous night|
|next week|the following week|
|here|there|
|this|that|
|these|those|

##### Example
- She said, “I will come tomorrow.”
- She said that she would come the next day.

## Yes or no questions
- Use _if_ or _whether_

## Wh-questions
- Keep the question word
- **Important**: Do _not_ use question word _order_ in reported speech.
## Commands and requests

Use to + verb.

|Direct speech|Reported speech|
|---|---|
|said|told / ordered / advised / requested|
##### Examples
- The teacher said, “Sit down.”
- The teacher told the students to sit down.
- Mother said, “Please help me.”
- Mother requested me to help her.

###### For negative commands
- “Do not waste time.”
- He advised me not to waste time.

### Exclamations

Remove the exclamation mark and use words like exclaimed with joy, sorrow, surprise, admiration, etc.

##### Examples
- She said, “What a beautiful flower!”
- She exclaimed with admiration that it was a very beautiful flower.
___
- He said, “Alas! I have lost my wallet.”
- He exclaimed with sorrow that he had lost his wallet.

#### **If the reporting verb is in the present or future tense, the tense usually remains the same.**
